# Methodology Appendix (Audit approach, rules, and scoring)

## A. Audit methodology (what was done)
1. **Artifact inventory and dependency mapping**
   - Identified all outputs in the provided context: baseline brief, product/service extraction, competitor segmentation, inferred BMC, validated/rejected assumptions, taxonomy normalization, comparison tables, geography matrix, scorecards, SWOT, risk/opportunity registers, strategic recommendations.
2. **Evidence traceability check**
   - For each major claim, checked whether it is explicitly supported by a URL/source contained in the context package.
3. **Inference leakage check**
   - Flagged areas where “reasonable” assumptions were presented with high confidence or as though validated (notably in the BMC), despite no supporting evidence in the provided context.
4. **Consistency check across outputs**
   - Verified that:
     - Legal identity details (CIN, address, name) remained consistent
     - Constraints (no pricing/compliance/partner/traction/integration assumptions) were consistently applied
     - “Unknown” handling was consistent across tables and narrative
5. **Executive readiness check**
   - Ensured the final consolidated view provides:
     - What is confirmed now
     - What is unknown
     - Risks created by unknowns
     - Clear next steps that do not require asserting new facts

## B. Evidence rules (hard constraints applied)
A statement may be treated as factual in this deliverable only if:
- It appears explicitly in the provided context text **and** is attributable to an evidence URL listed in the context, or
- It is a “validated assumption” about the artifact itself (e.g., “the artifact contains an inferred BMC”), not about the real-world company.

The following are **prohibited as factual** unless explicitly evidenced in the provided context:
- Pricing details (plans, amounts, free tiers, discounts)
- Compliance assertions (HIPAA, SOC2, ISO, etc.)
- Official partner status (e.g., Meta/WhatsApp BSP)
- Traction (customers, ARR/MRR, growth, adoption)
- Integration coverage (named integrations) unless explicitly cited

## C. Taxonomy/feature scoring methodology (as used in the provided outputs)
1. **Normalized capability dictionaries** were defined per category (WhatsApp automation, invoice OCR/IDP, expense splitting, services).
2. **Capability evidence coding:**
   - **Y (evidence):** explicitly stated in cited context URL(s)
   - **U (unknown):** not stated in cited context URL(s); must not be inferred
3. **Feature Evidence Score (0–100):**
   - Computed as: (# of Y capabilities / # of capabilities in that category) × 100
4. **Evidence Completeness (0–5):**
   - Qualitative rating of how richly the cited sources describe the offering (still evidence-bounded).
5. **Differentiation Potential (0–5) and GTM Clarity (0–5):**
   - Bounded to what is documented in the evidence pack; not a market-truth claim.
